Mic check (test page)

For the room-loop idea: this checks what your iPhone does when a web page opens the microphone while it plays sound. One tap runs three short tests (about 15 s). Turn the volume to a normal level, take headphones out, and keep the phone on the table.

Your browser will ask to use the microphone. The sound it hears stays in this tab only so you can replay it; nothing is saved or sent.
